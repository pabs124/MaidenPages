Score every ball in a scorer’s book.

Maiden: Cricket Scorer keeps ball-by-ball score for gully, tape-ball, backyard and club matches, drawn as a paper scorer’s book. Share the scorecard as an image. It works fully offline.

What you get

  • Ball by ball. Wides, no-balls, byes, leg byes, run outs, retired hurt and last man stands.
  • Your rules. Any number of overs and players a side.
  • Fix mistakes fast. Undo, or tap any ball in the book to edit it.
  • Shareable scorecard image when the match is done.
  • Fully offline. No account, no signal needed.

Maiden Pro

Scoring, all the rules and the share card are free. Maiden Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase: every match kept forever (free keeps your last 3), saved squads, season stats per player and extra scorebook finishes. It is not a subscription.

Private

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your matches stay on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

Short version: Maiden collects no data. It has no account or login, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Nothing is sent to any server.

What stays on your device

Your matches, including team and player names and every ball, are stored only on your iPhone in the app’s own storage. They are deleted when you delete the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

Sharing a scorecard

A scorecard image is only shared if you tap Share. It then goes through the iOS share sheet to the place you choose.

Live Activity

The optional Live Activity on your Lock Screen is shown by your device from local data. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Purchases

The optional Maiden Pro purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no payment details.

Children

Because Maiden collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email simplehelpdesk321@gmail.com.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account or login.

Does it work offline?

Yes, fully. Maiden needs no internet connection.

Where are my matches stored, and what happens if I delete the app?

Only on your device, in the app’s own storage. Nothing is sent to a server. If you delete the app, its matches are deleted too.

How do I fix a mistake?

Tap Undo to take back the last ball, or tap any ball in the book to edit it.

What rules does it support?

Wides, no-balls, byes, leg byes, run outs at either end, retired hurt and retired out, and last man stands. You can set any number of overs and players a side.

What does Maiden Pro include, and is it a subscription?

Maiden Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. It adds every match kept forever (free keeps your last 3), saved squads, season stats per player and extra scorebook finishes. Scoring, all the rules and the share card are free.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap Restore Purchase in the app, using the same Apple Account you bought with. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I share the scorecard?

When the match is done, tap Share on the scorecard and pick where it should go from the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share.
